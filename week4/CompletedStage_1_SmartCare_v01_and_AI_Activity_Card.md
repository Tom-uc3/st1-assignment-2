# SmartCare v0.1 \- Initial Engineering Brief and AI Activity Card

A2 Case Study Stage 1 student resource

# **SmartCare scenario**

SmartCare Community Clinic currently uses spreadsheets and paper records to manage patients and appointments. The client says: 'We need software to help manage patients, practitioners and appointments.' This is not yet a complete specification.

# **Initial Engineering Brief**

## **1\. Problem summary**

Write approximately 100 words.

Using spreadsheets and paper records creates unnecessary difficulty in figuring out whether a patient's files are on paper in a filing cabinet or in the spreadsheet. For appointments, this sometimes leads to duplicate bookings. It also makes it difficult to store and find any previous appointment history. 

IT also makes keeping records of when the practitioners are available difficult, leading to more appointment cancellations and rescheduling.

## **2\. Initial stakeholders**

| Stakeholder | Possible need |
| :---- | :---- |
| Doctor  | To be able to see their upcoming appointments and update some of their clients information. |
| receptionist | To add and remove appointments from the calendar and share them with the doctor and clients. To add clients' details to the software to be able to manage their information. |
| Smart care client | A phone app to book appointments and manage or view their information/doctors notes.  |
